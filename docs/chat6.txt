› what ae the decimal places we use


• In bn880_test.py, GPS is printed with 7 decimal places:

  lat_str = f"{g['lat']:12.7f}"
  lon_str = f"{g['lon']:13.7f}"

  So output looks like:

  47.5901234
  -121.9865678

  That is about 1 cm-ish precision on paper, but real GPS accuracy is much worse, usually around 1-3 meters outdoors for a BN-880 depending on signal.

  Other values:

  alt_str = f"{g['alt']:7.1f}"
  heading_str = f"{h:6.1f}°"

  So:

  - latitude: 7 decimals
  - longitude: 7 decimals
  - altitude: 1 decimal
  - heading: 1 decimal
  - satellite count: integer
