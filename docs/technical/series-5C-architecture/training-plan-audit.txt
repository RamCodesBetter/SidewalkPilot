WHAT CHANGED NOW
----------------
Decision 9 explicitly chose weighted focal as the baseline, comparing focal plus
Dice and retaining Dice only if frozen validation evidence improved. The previous
5.C callback instead inherited 5.B's weighted cross-entropy loss.

The new 5.C policy is 5.C-convnext-unet-fp32-focal-v2:
  * Focal is default. Focal + Dice is optional, not an asserted improvement.
  * Correct-class probability is derived from UNWEIGHTED cross-entropy. Class
    weighting is applied outside the focal factor, not inside exp(-weighted CE).
  * Strong and weak image means remain separate; weak mean has coefficient 0.5.
  * Dice averages background/sidewalk overlap per reviewed image, excludes 255,
    and does not use positive-only or automatic weak labels as full ground truth.
  * Focal gamma 1.5, class weights 2/1, cap 4, optional Dice coefficient 0.5 are
    explicit starting choices, NOT exact constants recovered from the old plan.
    Gamma matches the older S3/S4 default; its optimal value for S5 is untested.
  * Loss settings, actual class weights, loss and sampler hashes enter checkpoints
    and W&B metadata. Old runs cannot silently resume under a different loss.
  * Local and live W&B component metrics are wired in; no live upload occurred.
  * A/B objectives and artifacts are unchanged. New focal and focal+Dice runs
    need distinct output directories and the same frozen data/split/seed/budget.
  * Only synthetic unit/smoke checks ran, not full research-model training.
  * Later naming decision: 5.C = end, 5.Cb = best validation sidewalk IoU,
    5.Cs = reserved smart selection. Completed non-smoke runs publish the first
    two .pt snapshots and checkpoint-selection.json. Interrupted runs retain
    last.pt for resume; smoke runs are not promoted. No s checkpoint is created
    until a genuine smart-selection criterion exists. A/B artifacts stay intact.

HIGH-PRIORITY REMAINING FINDINGS
------------------------------
1. Final split is not implemented (Decision 4).
   Planned: 80/10/10 similarity groups, not whole recording runs.
   Current: enforced recording-DAY partitions; A has 247/118/33 images and B
   has 1212/118/33. A/B validation/test IDs are identical, with no exact B
   train/test ID overlap. This does not prove visual/geographic independence.
   Evidence: series_5_a_sidewalkpilot_trainer.py::validate_split/create_split;
   ~/sidewalkpilot-labels/series5-5.A-training/split.json and series5-5.B/split.json.
   These are disclosed development splits, not the final promised split. Do not
   silently reshuffle exposed examples and describe the result as untouched.

2. Encoder warmup and LR warmup are missing (Decision 9).
   AdamW, lower encoder LR, cosine decay, finite checks and gradient clipping
   exist. Both encoder and decoder are optimized from the first update; there
   is no staged encoder freeze/unfreeze or learning-rate warmup. Freezing old
   backbone BatchNorm statistics is not freezing weights; ConvNeXt uses LayerNorm.
   Evidence: series_5_a_sidewalkpilot_trainer.py::train/train_mode.
   Freeze duration and warmup schedule were not specified in the saved decision.

3. Sequence-aware sampling is missing (Decision 9).
   Current sampler cycles reviewed references and shuffles strong/weak examples.
   It does not reduce redundant neighboring-frame exposure through similarity
   or sequence groups. Label selection in chronological bins is not that sampler.
   Evidence: series_5_a_sidewalkpilot_trainer.py::MixedBatchSampler;
   series_5_b_sidewalkpilot_trainer.py::ScreenedBatchSampler.

4. Checkpoint score is still sidewalk IoU only (Decision 9).
   Planned score also considers critical-class recall, macro IoU, boundaries,
   false-drivable error and planner validity. Current best.pt/early stopping
   use only validation sidewalk IoU, with 13 fully binary validation references
   in the existing development set. Score weights/thresholds remain unspecified;
   the unavailable planner cannot honestly supply planner-validity metrics yet.
   Evidence: series_5_a_sidewalkpilot_trainer.py::train/evaluate;
   docs/site/research-context/series-5-accessibility-study.md (5.A development).

5. Deployment resolution/calibration are not qualified (Decision 7).
   Planned: calibrate/undistort the mounted 1280x720 view, compare 320x180 and
   512x288. Current 5.C default: 960x540, direct resize, no calibration applied
   by the labeling config. No final Jetson accuracy/latency resolution choice
   is established. Retain the beta setting; do not silently reduce resolution.
   Evidence: tools/configs/series_5_c_config.json;
   tools/configs/label_config_descriptive.json;
   series_5_a_sidewalkpilot_trainer.py::SidewalkDataset.

6. Existing test images were exposed through development viewers (Decision 18).
   A/B comparison viewers contain all 33 test images and identify development
   exposure. This does not establish that the user personally reviewed every
   one of the 33 images or that all masks are full binary references. These are
   from the 398-image A development dataset, not the final 10% of the full corpus.
   test_evaluated=false in a training run means only that its training
   loop did not evaluate test: it does NOT undo separate viewer inspection.
   Exact influence of each review is unknown. After test-informed changes, a
   genuinely unexposed final holdout is needed; provenance must survive that work.
   Evidence: ~/sidewalkpilot-labels/series5-5.B/5.A-vs-5.B/manifest.json;
   tools/datasets/prepare_series5_pseudo_dataset.py::load_mixed_partition.

7. Labels are below the requested 5.C gate (Decision 6 and later request).
   Campaign snapshot: 10025 processed unique images, 969 automatically accepted,
   9056 deferred, 9031 short of the 10000-candidate training goal. State is
   paused_after_bounded_run, not complete. These are automatic candidates, NOT
   969 reviewed gold masks. The corpus has 81237 files / 81231 unique images.
   The trainer's existing 10000-pseudo-label full-run gate remains in place.
   Evidence: ~/sidewalkpilot-labels/series5-5CD-labeling/progress.json,
   updated_utc 2026-11-22T00:18:45.230569+00:00.
   This audit does not resume that campaign or approve masks that failed checks.

8. C export/planning/runtime are unfinished, not secretly deployed (D10-D20).
   The existing exporter calls the A/B loader and tests CPU ORT on one random
   input. It is not a C exporter or the required Jetson CUDA-only equivalence
   and timing qualification. Existing vehicle inference permits CPU fallback.
   Evidence: tools/models/export_series5_models.py::export;
   code/controller/current/rc_car_app/jetson_inference_server.py.
   The versioned path protocol, metric free-space grid, clearance planner,
   Pure Pursuit, confidence-driven normal throttle and associated safety tests
   are not yet integrated into a Series 5 driving stack. No field claim follows
   from a binary mask, model name, or two-update smoke test.

DECISION-BY-DECISION STATUS
-------------------------
D1  Scope/timing: navigation and measurement separation remains a design contract.
    September targets are historical, not current deadlines. Latest conversation
    reports January 10 as experimentation end; this audit does not modify forms.
D2  Release identity: complete-stack versioning still pending. Newer explicit
    5.A/5.B/5.C beta naming overrides the older no-letter model-naming wording.
    Base/b/s now distinguish end, best-IoU, and reserved smart-selection states.
    This changes checkpoint naming, not architecture, optimizer, or selection score.
D3  Images/provenance: the 81237-file amendment supersedes 80969. Hash-linked
    immutable source provenance exists, but builders COPY JPEGs into frozen
    datasets despite the old literal no-duplication promise. Decide whether to
    document snapshot copies as an exception; do not delete valid snapshots.
    Evidence: tools/datasets/prepare_reviewed_dataset.py (copyfile),
    tools/datasets/prepare_series5_pseudo_dataset.py (copy2).
D4  Split: beta day grouping differs from final similarity-group 80/10/10.
D5  Taxonomy/policy: 12 classes supersede 16. Binary beta is intentional, not
    completion of 12-class training or a driving-policy table.
D6  Labeling: Grounded SAM 2 review rules remain the historical approved-plan
    description. SAM 3/pipeline trials are documented development, not proof
    of that procedure's completion or independently verified full-corpus masks.
    This audit makes no determination about subsequent fair amendments.
D7  Calibration/resolution: runtime full-FOV intent remains; calibration and
    smaller-resolution deployment benchmark remain unimplemented/unqualified.
D8  Architecture: ConvNeXt-Tiny pretrained encoder + modest U-Net-style decoder,
    skips and resized logits match the plan. Controlled MobileNet comparator
    remains unperformed: A/B used different data/resolution/conditions.
D9  Training: focal restored now. AdamW/cosine/finite checks exist. Warmup,
    sequence-aware sampling and final safety-aware checkpoint selection remain.
D10 Precision/export: NEW FP32-only decision supersedes AMP/FP16/INT8 ladder.
    FP32 C export, CUDA-required runtime and Jetson evidence remain pending.
D11 Decoding/confidence: argmax(logits) has the same winner as argmax(softmax).
    That is not a conflict. Retained probabilities, entropy/margins and corridor
    confidence products are missing. Temporal smoothing was conditional, not
    mandatory; do not add it without measured need and response-delay tests.
D12 Metric free space: class-policy map, vehicle-connected component, projection,
    inflation and free/occupied/unknown states are not integrated for Series 5.
D13 Planner: continuity-aware metric clearance planner not integrated. The plan
    did not require starting with A* or dynamic programming.
D14 Steering: Pure Pursuit, measured-wheelbase path conversion and versioned
    waypoints not integrated. Do not call the current steering-model runtime S5.
D15 Throttle: deterministic normalized 0..1 S5 policy not integrated. No learned
    throttle head is planned. Existing LiDAR-governed behavior is legacy control.
D16 Runtime: legacy 15-float steering response remains. Latest-frame replacement
    and freshness checks exist; versioned geometry/path checks and measured
    camera-to-hardware-command 50Hz qualification do not.
D17 Safety: existing manual/AEB/stale-result foundations do not establish the
    planned S5 invalid-path/geometry/version behavior. No explicit AEB-off
    would-have-intervened log was found. IMPORTANT: old AEB-off proposals must
    not override the later user-pasted research-plan requirement that AEB stay
    enabled. Source: Risk and Safety text in this conversation; NOT independently
    verified against the latest signed approval. Confirm the approved version
    before field changes; no signed-form reconciliation was performed here.
    No safety/controller code was altered in this task.
D18 Reports: binary P/R/F1/IoU/mIoU exist; full boundary, corridor, planner,
    clearance and end-to-end timing report remains incomplete. Final holdout
    needs special attention after development viewer exposure.
D19 Field sequence: recorded replay through staged supervised route validation
    is a plan, not evidence found in the current C smoke artifact. No new tests
    with people, altered AEB settings, or field operation authorized by this audit.
D20 Promotion: full-stack qualification and deployment evidence outstanding.
    Keep historical baseline artifacts; model-size or label-count alone is not
    a promotion gate. Current C local artifact is smoke-complete only.
